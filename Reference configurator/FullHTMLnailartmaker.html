Almond, Coffin, or Stiletto — Find the Perfect Nail Shape for Your Hands

The silhouette of your nails defines the styling direction of your hands. Different shapes alter the visual lines of your hand:

Almond Shape

Sophisticated & Chic

Mimicking the contour of a natural almond, this shape features gently tapered sidewalls that meet in a soft, rounded tip. It elongates the fingers instantly, making it a universal styling favorite.

Cat Eye, Matte, Gloss, Chrome & Velvet Nail Finishes Explained

The finish determines how light refracts off the nail plate. Choosing the right top coat or magnetic application alters the depth, hue, and dimensional quality of your color:

High Gloss

Classic, Juicy, Glass-Like

The ultimate salon standard. A glass-like top coat that maximizes light reflection, magnifying color pigments for a fresh-painted, juicy look.

💡
Pro Tip

Apply a thin layer of top coat every 3 days to revive the shine and protect against micro-scratches.

❌
Common Mistake

Never apply high gloss over uncured color gel, or it will create a dull, sticky finish.

✨

Did you know?

"High gloss top coats use highly reflective polymers that bounce light in parallel paths, simulating a wet surface."

Chapter 5

DIY Press-On Nail Guide: Make Custom Press-Ons Last 3+ Weeks

Longevity is 90% preparation. Skipping steps leaves moisture and oils trapped under the nail plate, causing pocketing and lifting. Follow this professional prep checklist:

01

Wash & De-grease

Wash hands with dish soap (de-greaser). Wipe each nail bed with a lint-free alcohol pad.

02

Treat Cuticles Gently

Push back the cuticle line with a wooden stick. Remove any dead skin on the plate.

03

Gently Micro-Buff

Buff the natural nail shine away with a 240-grit buffer to create microscopic grip.

04

Chemical Dehydration

Perform a final wipe with pure acetone or isopropyl alcohol to remove dust and moisture.

05

Secure Extension at 45°

Apply glue, press down starting at the cuticle at a 45-degree angle to squeeze out air.

💅

Nail Health Rule

"Never apply polish over oily nails without dehydrating first. Cleanse natural nail beds with pure isopropyl alcohol to remove all residue sebum."

💡 Pro Tip: Chemical Bonds

Natural nails are highly porous and release oils. Using a primer or dehydrator creates a chemical link between the natural keratin and the acrylic/gel adhesive, doubling your set's longevity.
